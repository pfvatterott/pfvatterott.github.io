Technical Depth Meets Client Execution

A proven hybrid engineer bridging deep codebase navigation, customer architecture, and product deployment.

I am a Forward Deployed Engineer, Solutions Engineer, and Customer Engineer based out of Salt Lake City, Utah. With a track record spanning PropelAuth, BetterCloud, and ClickUp, I thrive at the intersection of complex codebases, enterprise architecture, and customer engineering—where I can dive directly into client code, build custom SDK integrations, and ensure seamless technical deployment.

Whether acting as a Forward Deployed Engineer embedded with client teams, a Solutions Engineer designing API architectures, or a Customer Engineer solving mission-critical production blockers, I deliver speed, clarity, and technical excellence.
